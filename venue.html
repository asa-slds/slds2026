Venue

Conference Venue

The conference will be held at New York Marriott at the Brooklyn Bridge, Brooklyn, New York, USA

Address: 333 Adams Street, Brooklyn, NY 11201. See the official hotel website for hotel and arrival information.

Conference Floor Plan

Salon DE / Plenary comprises sections D and E of the Grand Ballroom; Salon F is section F. Select a map to view it at full size.

Getting to the Conference

  • Sunday, November 1, 2026 is NYC Marathon day, which coincides with the first day of the conference. Expect road closures and travel disruption in Brooklyn and elsewhere in the city. Allow extra time, especially if traveling by car, taxi, rideshare, or bus. Check NYC DOT traffic advisories before traveling. DOT also publishes marathon-related construction restrictions.
  • Plan your route in advance. Consider subway or rail travel where practical to reduce exposure to road closures. Check MTA service updates and travel information shortly before departure, including planned weekend service changes and elevator availability if needed.
  • Allow extra time for airport transfers. Check your route before leaving for the hotel or airport and build in time for traffic and transfers. The hotel does not provide an airport shuttle; see the hotel's transportation information when arranging your journey.

Hotel Reservations

The conference-rate booking deadline was October 12, 2026. For current room availability and rates, please contact the hotel directly or visit its official website.
